## 2. Verified Vector Verb Inventory & Selectional Restrictions

| Vector Verb | Root | Semantic / Aspectual Function | Selectional Restrictions | Attested Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ফেলা** (*phela*) | `fel-` | Telic completion, irreversible finality, abruptness. | Requires dynamic transitive or unergative pole; rejects stative verbs (*\*জেনে ফেলা). | *চিঠিটা লিখে ফেললাম* |
| **নেওয়া** (*neoa*) | `ne-` | Self-benefactive, internal absorption, reflexive benefit. | Requires agentive transitive pole verb. | *বইটা কিনে নিলাম* |
| **দেওয়া** (*dewa*) | `de-` | Other-benefactive, external transfer, outward action. | Requires transitive/transfer pole directed toward external recipient. | *তাকে খবরটা জানিয়ে দিলাম* |
| **উঠা** (*utha*) | `uth-` | Sudden inception, spontaneous eruption, exclamation. | Requires inchoative or emotional expression pole verbs. | *সে হঠাৎ হেসে উঠল* |
| **বসা** (*bosha*) | `bosh-` | Inadvertent, rash, ill-advised or obstinate action. | Requires volitional action pole verb. | *কথাটা বলে বসলাম* |
| **পড়া** (*porha*) | `por-` | Involuntary transition into state, sudden falling. | Requires non-volitional physiological or state transition pole verbs. | *বাচ্চাটা ঘুমিয়ে পড়ল* |
| **রাখা** (*rakha*) | `rakh-` | Anticipatory preparation, preservative enduring state. | Requires preparatory transitive pole verbs. | *খাবারটা ঢেকে রাখলাম* |
| **থাকা** (*thaka*) | `thak-` | Habitual duration, continuous static posture. | Requires durative or static posture pole verbs. | *সে পথ চেয়ে থাকে* |

## 3. Light Verb Constructions (LVCs)

| Light Verb | Transitivity / Valency Effect | Typical Nominal Hosts | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **করা** (*kora*) | Transitive agentive ($+1$ valency) | *কাজ*, *প্রশ্ন*, *সাহায্য*, *অপেক্ষা*, *বিশ্বাস* | *আমি তাকে সাহায্য করলাম* |
| **হওয়া** (*howa*) | Intransitive inchoative ($-1$ valency) | *শুরু*, *শেষ*, *ভুল*, *দেরি* | *সভা শুরু হলো* |
| **পাওয়া** (*paowa*) | Experiencer state (assigns Gen/Dat) | *ক্ষিদে*, *ভয়*, *লজ্জা*, *কষ্ট* | *আমার ভয় করছে / ভয় পেলাম* |
