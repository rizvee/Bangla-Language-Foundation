# Conversational Register, Social Deixis & Pragmatics in BDSB — BLF

## 1. Social Deixis & The Three-Tier Honorificity System
Bangladesh Standard Bangla (BDSB) organizes conversational interaction across three distinct social honorificity tiers. These tiers govern subject pronouns, second/third person verbal inflection, directive force, and politeness clitics.

| Tier | 2nd Person Pronoun | Verb Agreement | Imperative Suffix | Social Distance / Interactional Context |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Honorific (Hon)** | *আপনি* (*Apni*) | `-en` (*করেন*) | `-un` (*করুন*) | High social distance, respectful address to elders, formal/professional contexts, strangers. |
| **Familiar (Fam)** | *তুমি* (*Tumi*) | `-o` / `-e` (*করো*, *করবে*) | `-o` (*করো*) | Moderate distance, peers, colleagues, friends, family of equal or slightly younger age. |
| **Intimate (Int)** | *তুই* (*Tui*) | `-is` / `-li` / `-bi` (*করিস*) | `-Ø` / `-i` (*কর*, *করিস*) | Zero social distance, childhood peers, younger siblings, intimate solidarity. |

## 2. Orthographic & Syntactic Disambiguation: `কি` vs `কী`

| Feature | `কি` (*ki* with short i-kar) | `কী` (*ki* with long ee-kar) |
|---|---|---|
| **Syntactic Category** | Invariant Polar Question Particle | Substantive Interrogative Pronoun |
| **Semantic Function** | Asks for binary truth-value affirmation/negation. | Asks for the identity/content of an unknown entity. |
| **Position in Clause** | Typically immediately pre-verbal (*তুমি কি যাবে?*). | Fills argument NP/object slot (*তুমি কী খাবে?*). |
| **Case Declension** | Non-declinable (invariant *কি*). | Fully declinable: *কী* (NOM/ACC), *কিসের* (GEN), *কিসে* (LOC). |

## 3. Pragmatic Particles & Focus Clitics

| Particle / Clitic | Category | Pragmatic Function | Canonical Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-ই** (`-i`) | Restrictive Clitic | Exclusive focus (*X alone / precisely X*). | *আমিই যাবো* (*I alone will go*) |
| **-ও** (`-o`) | Additive Clitic | Inclusive focus (*X too / even X*). | *তুমিও যাবে* (*You too will go*) |
| **তো** (`to`) | Discourse Particle | Contrastive topicalization, shared presupposition. | *আমি তো বলেছি* (*As for me, I already said it*) |
| **না** (`na`) | Tag / Softener | Sentence-final confirmation tag or directive softening. | *যাবে না?* (*You will go, right?*) |
| **যে** (`je`) | Emotive Particle | Emotive assertion, evidential reminder. | *দেরি হয়ে গেল যে!* (*Look, it has gotten late!*) |
| **বা** (`ba`) | Dubitative Particle | Counter-expectation or alternative suggestion. | *সে বা কী করবে?* (*What else could he do?*) |

## 4. Pragmatic Dialogue Acts Taxonomy
BLF establishes a 17-act pragmatic intent taxonomy mapped to syntactic constructions and honorificity constraints (`DA-STATEMENT`, `DA-INFO-QUESTION`, `DA-YES-NO-QUESTION`, `DA-REQUEST-HONORIFIC`, `DA-REQUEST-FAMILIAR`, `DA-COMMAND-INTIMATE`, `DA-PROHIBITION`, `DA-OFFER`, `DA-SUGGESTION`, `DA-PERMISSION-REQUEST`, `DA-THANKING`, `DA-APOLOGY`, `DA-GREETING`, `DA-FAREWELL`, `DA-CONFIRMATION`, `DA-DISAGREEMENT`, `DA-CLARIFICATION`).
